How to connect

Join KENNY-Robot Wi-Fi and open http://192.168.4.1. Keep this page visible while driving. No internet connection is needed.

Leaving this page, losing the link or pressing STOP disarms the robot. Re-arm deliberately to continue. Reverse is enabled for manual control.

Scan ArUco · phone camera

Read marker ID/value and image coordinates. X increases right, Y increases down from the image’s top-left. Scanning stops and disarms driving.

Choose the dictionary used to print your markers.

These are image pixels, not map coordinates or distance in meters. ArUco stores a numeric ID; it does not directly encode a location. Photos are processed locally on your phone.

Bench-test with all wheels raised first. STOP removes drive power commands; wheels can coast. This page does not perform obstacle or cliff avoidance.
